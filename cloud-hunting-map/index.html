When are the clouds best?

  • Season — rainy months (about June–August) give the most clouds; cold mornings at the end of the year are also good
  • Time — be there 4:30–6:30, before and just after sunrise
  • Weather — best after a rainy night with still air
  • Not every day — on dry, windy mornings there may be no clouds at all

Tips for a dawn ride

  • Leave in the dark: ride slowly, lights on, and watch for fog.
  • Bring a warm jacket and gloves — mornings are cold.
  • Da Phu Hill: the last part is steep — park at the bottom and walk up.
  • Lang Biang's Radar Peak opens at 7:30 — go for morning views rather than sunrise.
