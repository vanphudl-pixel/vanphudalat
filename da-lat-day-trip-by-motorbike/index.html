Frequently asked questions

Is a self-guided Da Lat day trip by motorbike hard?

Not if you are used to an automatic scooter: follow Google Maps, ride slowly on passes and bends, wear a helmet and follow the traffic rules. Start early, especially in the rainy season, when rain usually comes in the afternoon.

How much does a bike cost for one day?

150,000 VND per day for any bike — Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade 125cc or an electric scooter — with a helmet and a phone holder. No cash deposit: you leave your passport or ID card.

Which route should I choose?

Love photos and want a compact day: the hot check-in route. With kids or keen on theme parks: the new attractions 2026 route.

Which route works on an electric scooter?

The check-in route: each electric scooter has 2 batteries (about 60–70 km), enough for the whole loop. The new attractions route is long and hilly — take a Honda Air Blade 125cc.
