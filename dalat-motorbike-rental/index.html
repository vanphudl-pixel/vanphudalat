Why explore Da Lat by motorbike

Da Lat is a mountain city on the Lang Biang plateau with a cool climate, pine forests, lakes and waterfalls. The main sights are spread over the hills around town and many aren't served by buses. With a bike you choose your own route, skip tours and taxis, and see far more in a day.

In one day you can ride around the center and Xuan Huong Lake, then reach Datanla Waterfall and Tuyen Lam Lake — and the next morning watch the sunrise over the Cau Dat tea hills. We've put our favorite routes on a separate page.

Motorbike rental in Da Lat: FAQ

How much does motorbike rental in Da Lat cost?

Any bike in our rental — Honda Vision, Honda Air Blade 125cc or an electric scooter — costs 150,000 VND per day. Fixed price, no hidden fees.

Do I need to leave a cash deposit?

No. There's no cash deposit: you leave your passport or ID card during the rental. The rental fee is paid in full upfront.

How is one rental day counted?

From pickup until 9:00 PM the same day. Returning by 9:00 PM the next day counts as 2 days, and so on.

Do you deliver to hotels?

Yes. For rentals of 2 days or more we deliver the bike free of charge to hotels in central Da Lat.

What's included with the bike?

A free helmet and a phone holder come with every bike, and gas scooters are handed over with 1 liter of fuel.

Which bike should I choose?

For town and short trips, the Honda Vision and electric scooters are light and quiet. For hills and trips out of town, such as the Cau Dat tea hills, take the Honda Air Blade 125cc — it has more power on climbs.

Can I book in advance?

Yes. Send your dates and hotel name on WhatsApp, Zalo or WeChat and we'll confirm and prepare the bike.

Where is the shop?

Van Phu is at 1/15/5 Thông Thiên Học, Da Lat. It's marked on Google Maps — see the map below.
