Good to know

  • Travel time — about 12–14 hours, longer with many stops
  • Departures — most buses leave Da Lat in the afternoon or early evening and reach Da Nang the next morning
  • Hoi An — some buses also stop in Hoi An — ask when you book
  • Shuttle — many companies pick up from central hotels — ask when you book

Tips

  • For a ride this long, ask whether the bus has an onboard toilet.
  • Keep your passport, phone and money with you, not in the luggage hold.
  • The air-conditioning is cold at night — bring a jacket.
  • On many cabin buses you leave your shoes at the door — wear socks.
