Good to know

  • Travel time — about 6–8 hours via Bao Loc Pass, longer with many stops
  • Shuttle — most companies run a shuttle van between central hotels and their station — ask when you book
  • Night buses — often arrive in Saigon around 4–5 am
  • Holidays — book a few days ahead at weekends and holidays

Tips

  • Arrive 30 minutes early; the shuttle may pick you up before the departure time.
  • Keep your passport, phone and money with you, not in the luggage hold.
  • The air-conditioning is cold at night — bring a jacket.
  • Several offices (5–6, 8, 11, 13) are close together near Pham Hong Thai Street.
