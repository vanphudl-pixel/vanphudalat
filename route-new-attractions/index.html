Suggested plan

  • 7:30 — pick up the bike at our shop or your hotel, ride to Langbiang Land
  • 10:00 — on to Mongo Land in Ta Nung — grass hills, slides, animals
  • 12:30 — lunch on the way back towards the centre
  • 14:00 — Hoang Long Ohayo on Mimosa Pass
  • 16:00 — pick strawberries at the farm, return the bike before 21:00

Good to know

  • The loop is about 43 km in a straight line, longer by road and hilly (Ta Nung, Mimosa Pass) — take a Honda Air Blade 125cc; one electric-scooter battery (about 35–40 km) is not enough.
  • Entrance tickets are sold at each gate; many include a drink (according to visitor reviews) — check prices on site.
  • Fill up before Ta Nung — few petrol stations on the pass.
  • The Ta Nung road and Mimosa Pass have many bends — ride slowly and never overtake on a bend.
