Motorbike Rental Price in Da Lat (2026): What You Really Pay

Before you rent a scooter in Da Lat, it helps to know exactly what the price covers. Here is how our pricing works, what's included and what to check at any rental shop.

Our price: 150,000 VND per day for every bike

At Van Phu, every bike costs the same: 150,000 VND per day. That's the Honda Vision 110, the Honda Air Blade 125 and our electric scooters. The price is fixed — no high-season surcharge and no hidden fees. There is also no multi-day discount, so the price is simple: number of days × 150,000 VND.

What's included

  • A helmet and a phone holder on every bike, free.
  • Gas scooters come with 1 liter of fuel — enough to reach the nearest gas station.
  • Free delivery to hotels in central Da Lat for rentals of 2 days or more.
  • A test ride and a short video of the bike's condition, filmed together before you leave.

Deposit: no cash

We don't take a cash deposit. You leave your passport or ID card with us and get it back when you return the bike. The rental fee is paid upfront.

How a rental day works

We open every day at 4:30 AM — handy if you want to catch sunrise. A rental day runs from the moment you pick up the bike until 9:00 PM the same day.

What you pay on top

The only extra cost is fuel for gas scooters: refill at any gas station, they're everywhere in town. Electric scooters need no fuel at all, but have a range of about 40 km per charge — see our electric scooter rental page.

How to avoid surprises at any rental shop

  • Ask what the deposit is and when you get it back.
  • Film the bike before riding — scratches, mirrors, lights.
  • Check brakes and tires; Da Lat has many steep hills.
  • Ask the exact return time and what happens if you're late.
  • Gas scooters require a driver's license; our electric scooters don't.
