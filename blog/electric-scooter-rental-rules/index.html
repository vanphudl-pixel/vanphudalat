Electric Scooter Rental Rules – Van Phu Da Lat

Our electric scooters are light, quiet and made for riding around Da Lat. These are the simple rules for renting one from Van Phu — please read them before you pick up your scooter.

Price and pick-up

  • 150,000 VND per day, paid in full at pick-up. No cash deposit.
  • Leave your original passport or ID card. You get it back when you return the scooter in the same condition.
  • Before you leave, take a test ride and we film the scooter's condition together (body, mirrors, lights, battery). We compare with this video at return.
  • Helmets and a phone holder are included.

Rental time

  • One rental day runs from pick-up until 21:00 the same day.
  • Returning at 21:00 the next day counts as 2 days, and so on.
  • Returning after 21:00 is charged as one extra day. Want to extend? Message us first.

Riding rules

  • Maximum 2 people, with a combined weight under 100 kg. No heavy loads.
  • Driver and passenger wear helmets at all times.
  • No alcohol or beer when riding.
  • Strictly follow Vietnamese traffic law: ride on the right, stop at red lights, no phone while riding.
  • Do not lend the scooter to anyone else.
  • Don't ride through deep water. In the rain, slow down — Da Lat's hills get slippery.
  • Park in a guarded parking lot and lock the scooter carefully.

Battery

  • One battery lasts about 35–40 km, and you start with a full one.
  • Need more? Bring the scooter to our shop and we swap in a full battery for 20,000 VND.
  • When about a third is left, head back or call us. Never run the battery flat — hills and two riders use more power.
  • Avoid long, steep climbs such as Prenn Pass, Mimosa Pass and the road up Lang Biang. For longer trips a Honda Air Blade 125cc is the better choice.
  • Don't use other chargers and don't remove the battery yourself.

Damage, loss and flat tyres

  • New damage compared with the pick-up video: you pay the actual repair cost.
  • Lost helmet, phone holder or key: you pay the replacement price.
  • Lost or stolen scooter: tell us and the nearest police station immediately. You pay the scooter's value as agreed at rental.
  • Flat tyre: stop, push the scooter to the nearest tyre repair shop and pay for the repair. Riding on a flat damages the rim, and you pay for that damage.
