Electric Scooter or Gas Scooter: Which One to Rent in Da Lat?

Both cost 150,000 VND per day, but they suit very different days. Here's how to choose between an electric scooter and a gas scooter in Da Lat.

The short answer

Staying in town and riding short distances? An electric scooter is quiet, easy and needs no license. Planning hills, passes or trips out of the city? Take a gas scooter, ideally the Honda Air Blade 125cc.

Electric scooter: pros and limits

  • No driver's license needed to rent one from us.
  • Quiet, no gears, no fuel stops.
  • Range of about 40 km per charge — enough for the center and nearby sights.
  • Low power: slow on steep hills and passes.
  • Two riders only if your combined weight is under 120 kg.

More details: electric scooter rental in Da Lat.

Gas scooter: pros and limits

  • Plenty of power for Da Lat's hills — the Air Blade 125cc is the strongest climber we have.
  • No range worries: refuel at any gas station.
  • Best for the Cau Dat tea hills, Lang Biang and waterfall loops.
  • A valid driver's license is required.

Which bike for which day

  • Center, lake, night market, cafés: electric or Honda Vision.
  • Datanla Waterfall, Linh Phuoc Pagoda: either works.
  • Tea hills, Lang Biang, Elephant Falls, Pongour: gas, preferably Air Blade 125cc.
