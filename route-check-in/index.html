Suggested plan

  • 8:00 — Lac Hu Co Tran — Asian-style old town with a lake and bridges
  • 9:30 — Memory Station (Tram Ky Uc) — rail track, small lake, lots of flowers
  • 12:00 — lunch in the centre; electric scooters can swap the battery at our shop (20,000 VND)
  • 13:30 — Suoi Binh Yen — stream and pine forest, food on site
  • 15:30 — Happy Hill — flower hill over Tuyen Lam Lake; return the bike before 21:00

Good to know

  • The loop is about 27 km in a straight line, longer by road — on an electric scooter swap the battery at lunch (one battery is about 35–40 km), or take a Honda Air Blade 125cc for comfort.
  • Every stop sells an entrance ticket with a drink or combo (according to visitor reviews) — check prices at the gate.
  • Happy Hill is very busy at weekends — go on a weekday or arrive early.
  • The access roads to Suoi Binh Yen and Happy Hill are narrow and steep in places — ride slowly, especially in the rain.
