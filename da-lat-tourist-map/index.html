6 motorbike routes around Da Lat

Route 1 · Central Da Lat

Whole loop: ≈ 11 km in a straight line · by road roughly 14–16 km · Time: Half a morning to half a day

The market, churches, old villas and the railway station all sit within about 2.5 km. Park at a guarded lot and walk between nearby sights; come back to the night market in the evening.

Best bike: 🔋 Electric scooter or Honda Vision. An electric scooter has 2 batteries, about 50–60 km in total — enough for the loop

Route 2 · South – Prenn Pass and Tuyen Lam Lake

Whole loop: ≈ 19 km in a straight line · by road roughly 24–28 km · Time: Half a day

A waterfall, a Zen monastery in the pines and Da Lat's biggest lake. Some climbs but short; go in the cool morning and finish with a lakeside coffee.

Best bike: 🔋 Electric scooter or Honda Vision. An electric scooter has 2 batteries, about 50–60 km in total — enough for the loop

    Datanla Waterfall
    Hours: 7:30–16:30
    Cascades in the forest and an alpine coaster.

    Hours: 7:00–17:00
    Zen monastery above Tuyen Lam Lake; dress modestly.
    📍 Directions
  3. 10
    Tuyen Lam Lake
    Hours: open area, any time
    Mist rising from the lake between pine hills.
    📍 Directions
  4. 11
    Clay Tunnel
    Hours: 7:00–17:00
    Giant red-clay sculptures next to Tuyen Lam Lake.
    📍 Directions

Route 3 · West – Van Thanh flower village and Ta Nung

Whole loop: ≈ 18 km in a straight line · by road roughly 23–26 km · Time: Half a day

Flower gardens, the flower village and eco-parks on the western hills. The road up to Ta Nung winds a lot — ride slowly and skip it in heavy rain.

Best bike: 🔋 Electric scooter or Honda Vision. An electric scooter has 2 batteries, about 50–60 km in total — enough for the loop

  1. 12
    Du Sinh Church
    Hours: view from outside (open for Mass times)
    Looks like a pagoda from outside; hilltop view, clouds below on misty mornings.
    📍 Directions
  2. 13
    Van Thanh Flower Village
    Hours: 7:00–17:00
    Flower fields and greenhouses just west of town.
    📍 Directions
  3. 14
    Fresh Garden
    Hours: 7:30–17:30
    Large flower garden in Van Thanh with a small zoo, fish ponds and a café. Entry includes a drink; buying online is often cheaper than at the gate.
    📍 Directions
  4. 15
    Fairytale Land
    Hours: 7:30–17:00
    Storybook garden with round Hobbit-style houses and the Vinh Tien wine cellar. Entry about 90,000 VND; wine tasting costs extra.
    📍 Directions
  5. 16
    Flower Plateau (Cao nguyên Hoa)
    Hours: 7:00–16:30
    Eco area on the Ta Nung hills with wooden houses in pine forest and flower gardens. The downhill go-kart track is the main draw.

Route 4 · North – Valley of Love and Lang Biang

Whole loop: ≈ 17 km in a straight line · by road roughly 22–25 km · Time: Half to a full day

A valley, viewpoint hills and Lang Biang Mountain in Lac Duong. Radar Peak is reached by jeep or on foot, not by motorbike.

Best bike: ⛽ Honda Air Blade 125cc. Long or hilly route, not suited to an electric scooter

  1. 17
    Valley of Love
    Hours: 7:30–17:00
    Lake, pines and flower gardens north of the city.
    📍 Directions

    Dreamy Hill (Mong Mo Hill)
    Hours: 7:30–17:30
    Garden hill of about 12 hectares with flowers and sculpted plants. Reviews put adult entry around 250,000 VND; best from November to June.
    📍 Directions
  3. 19
    Thien Phuc Duc Hill
    Hours: open area, any time
    Short, steep walk to a wide view over the city and pine hills; known for its lone pine.
    📍 Directions
  4. 20
    Lang Biang Mountain
    Hours: 7:30–17:00
    Wide view over the Lac Duong valley; steep road up.
    📍 Directions

Route 5 · East – Trai Mat and Cau Dat

Whole loop: ≈ 40 km in a straight line · by road roughly 53–61 km · Time: Dawn to noon

Linh Phuoc Pagoda, cloud-hunting hills and the Cau Dat tea hills. For the sunrise leave around 4:30–5:00 — the shop opens at 4:30 am.

Best bike: ⛽ Honda Air Blade 125cc. Long or hilly route, not suited to an electric scooter

  1. 21
    Linh Phuoc Pagoda
    Hours: daytime
    Mosaic dragon made of bottle and pottery shards.
    📍 Directions
  2. 22
    Hon Bo Hill
    Hours: open area, any time
    Highest hill east of town; sunrise and clouds over Thai Phien flower village. The last stretch is steep red dirt — walk it.
    📍 Directions
  3. 23
    Cau Dat Tea Hills
    Hours: 6:00–18:00
    Rolling tea rows with clouds in the morning; the hill opens at 6:00.
    📍 Directions
  4. 24
    Cau Dat cloud-hunting spot (Thien Duong San May)
    Hours: 4:30–18:00
    Paid viewpoint that opens at 4:30; guests mention a ticket with a drink and breakfast.
    📍 Directions

Route 6 · Far south – Nam Ban and Pongour Falls

Whole loop: ≈ 70 km in a straight line · by road roughly 91–105 km · Time: Full day

Linh An Pagoda, Elephant Falls and Pongour Falls — the longest loop. Start early, fill up before you go and be back before dark.

Best bike: ⛽ Honda Air Blade 125cc. Long or hilly route, not suited to an electric scooter

  1. 25
    Linh An Pagoda (Nam Ban)
    Hours: 5:00–17:00
    Next to Elephant Falls; you can climb inside the tall Buddha statue.
    📍 Directions
  2. 26
    Elephant Falls (Nam Ban)
    Hours: —
    Wide, powerful falls next to Linh An Pagoda; steep path and wet rocks. Recent reviews say the falls are sometimes closed — ask before you go.
    📍 Directions
  3. 27
    Pongour Waterfall
    Hours: 7:30–17:00
    The widest falls around Da Lat, water over many rock terraces; about 1–1.5 hours each way on the highway.
    📍 Directions

1–4 day plans built from the routes

  • 1 day: Morning: Route 2 (south). Afternoon: Route 1 (centre). Evening: night market. →
  • 2 days, 1 night: Day 1 as above. Day 2: dawn on Route 5 (Cau Dat sunrise), relaxed afternoon in the centre. →
  • 3 days: Add a day: Route 4 (north) in the morning and Route 3 (west) in the afternoon — they are next to each other. →
  • 4 days: Keep the fourth day for Route 6 (Nam Ban, Pongour Falls).

Don't combine

  • Route 5 (Cau Dat, east) and Route 6 (Pongour, south) on the same day — opposite directions and a very long ride.
  • Route 5 and Route 3 (Ta Nung, west) in the same half day — you would cross the whole city.
  • Hilly routes in heavy rain or thick fog late in the evening.

Quick answers

Is there a printable Da Lat tourist map (PDF)?

Yes. Every theme map below (photo spots, waterfalls, pagodas, churches, cloud hunting, view cafés…) has a free PDF to print or save on your phone.

Which routes work on an electric scooter?

Route 1 · Central Da Lat, Route 2 · South – Prenn Pass and Tuyen Lam Lake, Route 3 · West – Van Thanh flower village and Ta Nung. Each electric scooter has 2 batteries, about 50–60 km in total, with a battery swap at the shop for 20,000 VND. The other routes are long or hilly — take a Honda Air Blade 125cc.

Which route is the longest?

Route 6 · Far south – Nam Ban and Pongour Falls: the loop is about 70 km in a straight line and longer by road. Plan a full day.

I only have one day in Da Lat — which route?

Ride Route 2 in the morning (Datanla Waterfall, Truc Lam Monastery, Tuyen Lam Lake), stroll Route 1 in the centre in the afternoon and end at the night market. Short distances, fine on an electric scooter.

How much is a rental at Van Phu?

150,000 VND per day for every bike (Honda Vision, Air Blade, electric scooter), free helmet, no cash deposit — you leave your passport or ID card. Free delivery to hotels in central Da Lat for 2+ days.
