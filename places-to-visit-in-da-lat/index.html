Quick answers

What should I see in Da Lat in one day by motorbike?

In the morning, Xuan Huong Lake, Da Lat Market and Crazy House in the centre; in the afternoon ride south to Datanla Waterfall, Tuyen Lam Lake and Truc Lam Monastery (about 5–7 km). With 2–3 days, add sunrise at the Cau Dat tea hills and Lang Biang Mountain.

Where should I go in Da Lat when it rains?

In the rainy season (roughly May–November) rain usually comes in the afternoon: ride to far places in the morning, then pick covered places near the centre such as the Bao Dai Summer Palace, the railway station, Linh Phuoc Pagoda or a café with a view. Avoid waterfalls and passes in heavy rain.

When is the best time to visit Da Lat?

The dry season (December–April) brings sunny days and dry roads, good for long rides. For flowers: wild sunflowers late October–December, pink grass November–December, cherry blossoms late January–February. The 11th Da Lat Flower Festival is planned for 19–31 December 2026.
